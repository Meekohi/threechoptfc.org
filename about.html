About the Club

Three Chopt Flying Club, LLC is a social flying club operating out of the Charlottesville-Albemarle Airport (KCHO). We were founded with the purpose of creating an opportunity for local pilots to come together and share their enthusiasm and interest in aviation.

We are a non-profit 501(c)(7) social club whose members fly together and attend social events together. We have Regular Members, who have paid more in order to fly the aircraft, and an Associate Member level for family members and social members. We work to limit our Regular Membership to a number that results in around 200 hours per year on each aircraft — roughly half what a rental fleet sees — which keeps the airplane available and in better condition.

We hold cookouts the first Saturday of each month from 11:30am–1:30pm outside of the Charlottesville Flight Center at the airport. Prospective members are welcome to stop by.
