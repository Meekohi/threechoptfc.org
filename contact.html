Where to Find Us

Our aircraft is hangared on the south side of the Charlottesville-Albemarle Airport (KCHO).

Most of our meetings and our monthly cookouts take place at the Charlottesville Flight Center:

200 Aviation Dr
Charlottesville, VA 22911

We hold a cookout the first Saturday of each month from 11:30am–1:30pm outside the Flight Center. Prospective members are welcome — come say hello.
