Where to find us

Our aircraft is hangared on the south side of Charlottesville-Albemarle Airport (KCHO).

Most of our meetings and our monthly cookouts take place at the Charlottesville Flight Center:

200 Aviation Dr
Charlottesville, VA 22911
Opens in Google Maps

There is parking at the Flight Center, and you can walk in through their door — you do not need to go through the main airport terminal or onto the ramp.

We hold a cookout the first Saturday of each month from 11:30am–1:30pm outside the Flight Center. Prospective members are welcome — come and say hello. You do not need to arrange anything in advance, but if you email us first we will make sure someone is looking out for you.
