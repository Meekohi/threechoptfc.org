Membership Information

Becoming a Member

We are currently accepting applications for new members. To join the Club, you make a joining contribution (refundable in most cases) and pay an annual fee for aircraft fixed costs. Regular Members can pay the annual fee in monthly increments.

Regular Plus Member

Voting rights and full aircraft access.

  • Refundable*: One-time $8,000 Membership Deposit
  • Non-Refundable: $90.83 / month membership fee

Regular Basic Member

Voting rights and full aircraft access.

  • Refundable*:
    • One-time $500 Membership Deposit
    • $25 / month loan principal up to $8,000 (can pay off early if desired)
  • Non-Refundable:
    • $90.83 / month membership fee
    • 4.5% APR of outstanding principal balance each month (starts at $28.13 / month and decreases over time)

Associate Member (Social / Family / Student)

No voting rights and cannot solo aircraft.

  • Refundable*: $20 Membership fee
  • Non-Refundable: $40 / year

* Principal Refund Policy: The bylaws outline this specifically, but basically you can get your "refundable" amount back as long as the club can afford it, or are put in a queue waiting to be paid out when a new member joins to replace you.

Regular Member Requirements

Regular Members have one vote and are allowed to fly our aircraft. To become one you must:

  • Hold at least a private pilot certificate
  • Hold a current medical certificate
  • Pass an interview with our Board of Directors

Checking Out in the Mooney

To solo N3275F you need:

  • 100 hours total time
  • A Club instructor sign-off
  • Either 20 hours retractable-gear time plus 5 hours in the Mooney, or 10 hours in the Mooney

You can receive instruction in the Mooney before meeting these minimums, including working on your instrument rating. Several of our members joined with fewer than 100 hours and built the required time while earning their instrument rating in the Mooney.

Associate Members

Associate Members participate in our social activities but cannot vote or solo Club aircraft.* You need only a desire to share your interest in aviation and a recommendation for membership from a Director.

If you do not yet have your private pilot certificate but are interested in the Club, we encourage you to join as an Associate Member while you train with one of the local flight schools on their aircraft. Contact us if you would like information about local flight schools.

* Family Student Pilots may use Club aircraft, but their use is limited per the Operating Agreement.

How to Join

We are currently interviewing prospective members. If this sounds like something you would like to do, get in touch — we will match you with a sponsor who will take you through the process.

  1. Email us to express interest
  2. We match you with a member sponsor
  3. Meet the Club — a social hour or a visit to the hangar
  4. Interview with the Board of Directors
  5. Review and sign the Operating Agreement and membership application
  6. Pay your membership deposit and first dues
  7. Complete your aircraft checkout with a Club instructor
